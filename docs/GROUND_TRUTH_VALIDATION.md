# Ground-truth validation (pre-publication audit)

The benchmark's ground truth is the frozen conventional delay-and-sum reference
(`cam1k_eval/reference.py`) evaluated on the standardized captures. This document
records the independent checks establishing that it is *the* ground truth — anchored
to physics and to the upstream data — and not an artifact of our own pipeline.

## 1. Closed-loop localization at known directions

Synthetic plane waves injected at six known (azimuth, elevation) pairs; the reference
must place the peak on the grid ray nearest the injected direction.

| az | el | band | peak pixel | expected pixel | angular error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +19 | −7 | 1 kHz | (15, 23) | (15, 23) | 0.50° |
| −24 | +5 | 315 Hz | (9, 6) | (9, 6) | 0.77° |
| −9 | +12 | 4 kHz | (5, 12) | (5, 12) | 0.50° |
| 0 | 0 | 1 kHz | (11, 16) | (11, 15) | 1.64° |
| +25 | +15 | 2 kHz | (3, 26) | (3, 26) | 0.56° |
| −30 | −15 | 2 kHz | (20, 3) | (20, 3) | 0.78° |

Nearest-pixel localization at every direction (angular error at most 1.64°); signs confirm the brief's conventions
(azimuth > 0 → image-right columns, elevation > 0 → upper rows).

## 2. Independent reimplementation (different algorithm, same physics)

A time-domain delay-and-sum (fractional-delay interpolation + band-passed power —
sharing no code path with the frequency-domain reference) reproduces the reference on
**real** capture data:

| band | correlation (dB maps) | peak pixels |
|---|---|---|
| 891–1122 Hz | **+0.9995** | identical (23, 31) |
| 2239–2818 Hz | **+0.9996** | identical (19, 17) |

Sensitivity control: inverting the alignment sign in the check collapses agreement to
−0.21 / +0.61 with mirrored peaks — i.e., this check would have caught a sign error
in the reference if one existed.

## 3. Raw-data interpretation

- Sample width and endianness are forced by byte-count arithmetic against the
  metadata's duration and sample rate.
- Channel-major layout: per-channel spectra carry 99.7% of energy above 100 Hz with
  structured acoustic peaks; the interleaved misreading yields 60.2% aliased rumble.
- `channelOrdering` direction: the chosen reading (`positions[ordering]` per stream
  row) produces phase-coherent focusing on real data (peak/median contrast 9.5 at
  1 kHz) vs 3.5 for the inverse and 2.5 unpermuted. 1,024 phases cannot conspire to
  focus under a wrong pairing.

## 4. Pipeline integrity

- The shipped public anchor is **byte-identical** to a fresh recompute from the raw
  1.92 GB upstream `sound` file (verified end-to-end after all refactors).
- All 32 SHA-256 hashes in the private manifest (sound + reference per case) verify.
- Upstream source files are pinned by size and SHA-256 in `constants.py`.
- Both captures report identical microphone geometry (same physical device).

## 5. External (photographic) consistency

The dataset ships the co-mounted camera's video (not exposed to candidates). Projected
through the shared pinhole model (with the 0.36 m camera-below-array parallax at the
10 m scene distance ≈ 2.1° correction):

- `axcar3` ("outside-engine"): the 2.5–4 kHz ground-truth hotspot lands beside the
  bright central doorway of an otherwise empty hall — the entry path for the engine
  noise of the car parked outside. See `docs/overlay_axcar3.png`.
- `axcar1` (`car=on`): the dominant 1 kHz source points to the lower-right frame edge,
  consistent with a running vehicle near the rig but outside the camera FOV. See
  `docs/overlay_axcar1.png`.

A corrupted ground truth (misparse, mirror, wrong ordering) produces grid-artifact
maps of the kind the evaluated models emitted — not spatially coherent lobes that
track scene openings.

## Scope statement

"Ground truth" here means the frozen conventional far-field DAS output — the
replication target that the brief's (fictional) vendor-export framing defines; the
anchor itself is generated by this repository's reference, as disclosed in the README.
Window/DC/edge-convention variants agree with it to within 0.1% in raw dB-map
correlation; on the published skill metric, which renormalizes by floor headroom,
those benign variants score 0.85–1.00. The anchor is the benchmark's declared
conventional-DAS target: the independent checks above establish that it is physically
consistent and free of the tested parsing, sign, orientation, and localization
errors; they do not claim a unique physical ground truth.
